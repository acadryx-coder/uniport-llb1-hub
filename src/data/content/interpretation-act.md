# Interpretation Act

:: The Interpretation Act supplies standard definitions and construction rules that apply across Nigerian federal legislation unless a particular statute says otherwise.

## Section 2 — Interpretation

§ Section 2(1)
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires —

- **"enactment"** means an Act or an Ordinance, and includes any subsidiary instrument made under an Act or Ordinance;
- **"court"** means any court of competent jurisdiction in Nigeria;
- **"person"** includes a body of persons corporate or unincorporate;
- **"prescribed"** means prescribed by the enactment in question or by subsidiary legislation made under it.

§ Section 2(2)
Where an enactment confers power to make subsidiary legislation, that power extends to making provision for all matters necessary or convenient for carrying the enactment into effect.
